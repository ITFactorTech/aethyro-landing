Trust & Security

What actually happens
to your data.

Not a compliance badge wall — Aethyro is solo-built and doesn't hold certifications it hasn't earned. This page is the specific, checkable truth about how your data is stored, secured, and deleted, written by the person who built it.

🗑️ Verifiable deletion

Delete a conversation and Aethyro doesn't just remove it from your screen — the database deletes the rows, then builds a receipt describing exactly what was removed and signs it server-side (HMAC-SHA256) with a key that never leaves the database. You get the payload, the signature, and a "Verify" button that independently recomputes the signature to confirm the receipt is genuine and hasn't been altered.

Why this matters: most apps' "delete" is a promise you can't check. This one gives you an artifact — a signed record — instead of just a toast notification that disappears. You can download it as JSON and keep it.

Try it: sign in, delete any conversation, and the receipt modal appears immediately with a working Verify button.

🔒 Account & access security

  • Row-level security on every table, enforced by the database itself, not just app code — your conversations, memory, and credit history are scoped to your account at the Postgres layer, so a bug in the frontend can't leak someone else's data.
  • No standing admin access. Admin dashboard functions check membership in a table with zero client-readable policies — becoming an admin requires a direct database insert, not a hardcoded email or a flag anyone can flip.
  • API keys are hashed, never stored in plaintext. A key is shown to you exactly once, at creation. Aethyro's own database only ever holds its SHA-256 hash — if our database were ever read by someone unauthorized, your key still couldn't be reconstructed from it.
  • Leaked-password check at signup. Your password is checked against HaveIBeenPwned's breach database using k-anonymity (only a 5-character hash prefix ever leaves your browser — the real password never does) before your account is created.
  • HTTPS everywhere, on every request, with no exceptions.

💳 Payments

Checkout is Stripe-hosted. Aethyro's own servers never see, touch, or store your card number — a purchase becomes a credit grant on your account via a signed webhook, and that's the only thing that crosses back into our database.

🧠 What your conversations are used for

Messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate your response, and for nothing else. Anthropic's API terms are a no-training policy on API inputs — your conversations aren't used to train Claude or any other model. Aethyro doesn't sell, share, or otherwise monetize your conversation content.

🏗️ Infrastructure

The frontend runs on Cloudflare Workers; data, auth, and access control run on Supabase/Postgres. Both are established infrastructure providers Aethyro builds on top of — this page describes what Aethyro itself does with your data on that infrastructure, not a claim about their own certifications.

What Aethyro deliberately doesn't do

  • Doesn't sell or share your data with third parties, ever.
  • Doesn't train AI models on your conversations.
  • Doesn't run a subscription you have to remember to cancel — credit packs are one-time purchases that never expire and never auto-renew unless you explicitly turn on auto-topup.
  • Doesn't claim certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.) it doesn't hold. If that ever changes, this page will say so specifically, not with a badge.
Honest about scale:

Aethyro is built and operated by one person. There's no dedicated security team, no bug bounty program (yet), and no 24/7 incident response. What's above is real and checkable, not aspirational — but if you're deciding whether to trust this with something genuinely sensitive, weigh that against what a larger team could offer.
