Common questions

Do I need a powerful computer?
Any modern laptop or desktop with 8GB RAM works. An NVIDIA GPU with 8GB+ VRAM gives the best performance, but Aethyro runs fine on CPU-only machines for most tasks.
What happens after the 14-day trial?
You're charged the monthly plan price automatically. You can cancel any time before the trial ends with zero charge — no calls required, just cancel in your dashboard.
Is there a usage limit on the local AI?
No. Local inference on your hardware is unlimited — you can run as many queries as you want, 24/7. The only limits are your hardware's speed.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes, any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is this HIPAA or attorney-client privilege compliant?
Aethyro doesn't automatically make you compliant — that still depends on your own policies, staff training, and any required agreements. What Aethyro does provide: all AI processing happens locally on your hardware, so client, patient, or legal data is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party AI provider. That removes the biggest compliance risk (external data transmission) but doesn't replace the rest of your compliance program. The Professional plan is built for firms working with this kind of sensitive data.
What does "self-improving" mean?
Aethyro evaluates its own performance over time and refines its behavior based on how you actually use it — on your hardware, with your local data patterns. It gets smarter the longer you use it, without any of your data leaving your machine.
