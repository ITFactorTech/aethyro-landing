Frequently asked questions

Can I use Claude without paying $20 a month?
Yes. Aethyro gives you Claude models in the browser on a pay-as-you-go basis. You buy credit packs (from $4) instead of a monthly plan, and every new account gets 200 free credits.
Do the credits expire?
No. Credits never expire, and nothing renews automatically, so a quiet month costs you nothing.
How many messages do I get?
It depends on the model and message length. A quick Haiku answer costs about 1 credit; a long Claude Opus 5.5 reply costs about 15. The 200 free credits cover roughly 13 long Opus replies or about 200 quick answers.
When is a subscription the better deal?
If you use Claude heavily every day, a flat subscription can work out cheaper. Pay-per-use wins when your usage is occasional or uneven, which is most people. The calculator on our homepage shows your likely monthly cost.
Is my data used to train models?
Aethyro never sells your data. Requests go to Anthropic's API to generate your reply (or to OpenAI's API if Claude is unavailable or you choose the GPT model); see our trust page for exactly how data is handled.
