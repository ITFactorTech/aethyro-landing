Yes. Aethyro uses Anthropic's official API. You're talking to the same model as the direct API, with no wrapper or fine-tuning applied.
What is a credit?
Credits are Aethyro's billing unit. One typical message costs 1–5 credits depending on its length and the length of the conversation history. The starter pack gives you 200 credits for $4.
Do credits expire?
No. Credits never expire. Buy a pack once and use it over weeks or months.
What's the context window?
Claude Opus 5 supports a 200k-token context window. Aethyro passes the full conversation history up to that limit on every request.
